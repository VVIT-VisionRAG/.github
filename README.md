# VVIT CSE AIML — Visual RAG Research

Welcome to the research organization of our B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) project team from
Vasireddy Venkatadri Institute of Technology (VVIT).

## Research Domain

Our research focuses on the intersection of:

- Computer Vision
- Visual Retrieval-Augmented Generation (Visual RAG)
- Retrieval
- Prompt Engineering
- Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)

## Research Direction

Our current research direction investigates training-free Visual RAG and whether
selecting visual evidence according to its discriminative usefulness can improve
multimodal reasoning compared with conventional similarity-based retrieval.

Our proposed research framework is currently referred to as:

**Con-VRAG**
